Morning on the farm

Water the vegetable beds, pick your greens and collect an egg. Bring the ingredients together in your hands, then lift your dish from the wok when the cooking ring turns green.

Golden hour at the beach

Scoop a red snapper with your net or dig a crab out of the sand. Gather seaweed, fire up the grill and carry the finished dish to the beach stall.
